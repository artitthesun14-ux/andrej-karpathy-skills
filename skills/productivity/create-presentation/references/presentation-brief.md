# Presentation Brief

One brief per job. Pass it between steps; save it as `presentation-brief.md` next to a medium or large deck. Write `unknown` for anything not established. Never guess a value to complete the form.

```
task_type: new | new-styled | transform | continue
user_goal: what the deck must achieve, in one sentence
audience: who watches, what they know, what they must decide or do
content_sources: files and messages that are the source of truth (path or description)
storyline: numbered slides, each with its job and form (from visual-storytelling), or "existing"
design_direction: the block from style-direction.md > Write the design direction, or "existing: <where it is defined>"
selected_style: style id(s) with primary and secondary roles, or "custom: <description>"
selected_palette: palette id, or custom colors with roles and the contrast check result
animation_requirements: what moves and why, or "none"
technical_constraints: format (HTML deck, single file, PDF export), devices, offline, libraries allowed, deadline
preserve: content, data, layouts, and features that must not change (routes C and D)
change_scope: exactly what may change (routes C and D)
deliverables: files to produce or edit, with paths
validation_criteria: how this job is judged done (renders at which widths, navigation, contrast, reviewed slides)
```

Update the brief when a decision changes. If the person overrides a value, record their value, not the earlier default.
