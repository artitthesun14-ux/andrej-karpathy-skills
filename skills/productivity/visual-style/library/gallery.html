Visual Style Library

Find a visual style, choose your colors, and apply them to your next project.

Styles are the look and the rules: type, layout, composition, image, motion. Any palette can go on any style. Choose a style and colors in any order; one of the two is enough to apply.

Colors are palettes with roles and checked contrast. They are separate from styles, so pick them in any order.

Ready-made palettes from the library. Every HEX value, name, and role below comes from the library data.

Tap a color to search with it. Library palettes that use it, or a close color, appear below, then palettes generated from it. Tapping only searches: your selected colors change when you press Select palette or Use these colors.

Enter your own color

Start from one color and a color-theory rule. Hues turn in OKLCH, so steps look even. Lock a color to keep it while you regenerate or adjust; type over any HEX and the computed value stays visible. Contrast ratios are calculated, not estimated.
